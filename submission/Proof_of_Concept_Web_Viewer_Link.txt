Project: Topoloka — Indoor Twins in a Link
Competition: Namma Space — Techfest 2026-27 (IIT Bombay)
Deliverable: Live Web App Link (Round 2)

Working Web Viewer URL:
https://sciencebanda09.github.io/topoloka/

Active Self-Captured Space (`home_v1`):
- Reconstructed strictly from the team's own 3-room flat walkthrough video (Entry Study Room, Central Hall, and Master Bedroom loop — all third-party/public datasets purged).
- Zero-install static WebGL viewer (GitHub Pages) streaming an instant colored 3D point cloud preview before swapping seamlessly into compressed Level-1 `.ksplat` 3D Gaussian Splats (11.55 MB, 480,000 Gaussians) at ~51.5 FPS on hardware WebGL (0 console/HTTP errors).
- Free first-person roaming (WASD/arrows, pointer-lock mouse look, mobile touch stick) with captured-VFOV shell clamping, 5 cm metric costmap wall collision (22.21 m^2 walkable), and a live 2D metric minimap (`M`).
- Spatial + fuzzy POI search (`/`, 1 m spatial buckets + inverted token + Levenshtein <= 2) across 9 validated POIs in 4 categories (e.g., typo "wardobe" -> "Wooden Wardrobe"), clickable 3D & minimap markers, explicit "Jump here" (`?poi=`), and "Navigate" (VCCW/EDT A* floor pathfinding with landmark-anchored turn-by-turn guidance, ETA, and off-route rerouting; e.g., Entry Study Desk -> Bedroom Alcove 9.8 m).
- Interactive Sandbox (`G`) to place physical props (Cone, Barrier, Crate, Sign) that immediately block the costmap and trigger live loop rerouting (verified: 6.5 m -> 7.7 m Central Hall -> Bedroom Alcove around the Dressing Mirror leg).
- In-browser click-floor POI tagging/editing (`E`) with localStorage persistence and contract-validated `pois.json` export.
- R3 Bonus AR Wayfinding Mode (`AR`) supporting live camera feed + QR-anchor (`anchors.json`) relocalization and directional turn overlays.
